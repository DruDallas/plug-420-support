Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026

Overview

Plug 420 is a private, activation-controlled record and workflow organizer. The current iOS release does not operate a developer-hosted service for app records and does not sell personal information.

Data you choose to store

The app can store records you enter or import, including contacts, inventory, money records, orders, deliveries, meeting spots, messages, history, settings, and activation information. These records are kept in protected app storage on the device. Protected import and export features operate only when you choose a file or sharing action.

Device features

  • Contacts: Contact access is used only when you choose to import a phone contact and grant iOS permission.
  • Maps and directions: When you choose a map or directions action, the meeting-spot text can be passed to the mapping or sharing service you select. That service's privacy policy then applies.
  • Files and sharing: You control protected file imports, exports, and shares through iOS.

Tracking, advertising, and purchases

The current iOS release does not include third-party advertising, cross-app tracking, analytics SDKs, in-app purchases, or a public social feed.

Security and activation

App records are encrypted in protected app storage. Access requires a signed, unexpired activation supplied by the app owner. No security measure can guarantee absolute protection, so users should protect their device passcode and exported files.

Deleting data

You can use the in-app Settings page to delete all app-owned records, activations, encrypted key stores, the encrypted vault, and the app-owned Keychain key. Deleting the app through iOS also removes its application container, subject to Apple's device and backup behaviour.

Children

Plug 420 is not designed as a children's service and does not knowingly collect children's information through a developer-hosted service.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, email drudallas@hotmail.com. Do not send passwords, encryption keys, activation files, or private records by ordinary email.

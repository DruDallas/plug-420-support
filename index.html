Plug 420 Support

Support for the private, owner-controlled Plug 420 record and workflow organizer.

Getting access

Plug 420 requires a signed, unexpired activation supplied by the app owner. The App Store application does not include an activation generator.

Contact support

Email drudallas@hotmail.com with the app version, the page you were using, and a description of the problem. Do not email activation files, encryption keys, passwords, or private records unless support specifically requests a safe transfer method.
